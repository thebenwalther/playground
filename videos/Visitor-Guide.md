# A weekend in Santa Ynez Valley

The revised 60-second video is a 1280 × 720, 16:9 film with full-screen modeled 3D scenes, moving cameras, dimensional titles, captions, and an original instrumental soundtrack. The scenes are artistic interpretations, not exact landmark reconstructions or aerial footage.

## Where to visit

| Stop | What makes it worth visiting |
| --- | --- |
| **Santa Ynez** | Western-style downtown architecture, relaxed restaurants, and a pleasant small-town starting point. |
| **Ballard** | A quiet village detour for a bakery stop and country atmosphere. |
| **Solvang** | Danish-style architecture, windmills, bakeries, aebleskiver, and Old Mission Santa Inés. A strong first stop for a visitor. |
| **Los Olivos** | Walkable wine tasting rooms, galleries, and patios. Reserve specific tastings and arrange a sober driver. |
| **Buellton** | Restaurants, craft beer, and nearby Ostrichland USA, where ostriches and emus make a memorable family outing. |
| **Lake Cachuma** | Boating, birdwatching, lakeside walks, and mountain views. Swimming and other direct body-contact recreation are prohibited. Check current access and rental availability. |

## A relaxed two-day route

**Day 1 — Towns and food:** Begin with breakfast and a downtown walk in Santa Ynez. Spend late morning and lunch in Solvang, with time for a bakery and Old Mission Santa Inés. Continue toward Buellton for Ostrichland USA and dinner. Check the attraction's closing time before setting the day's order.

**Day 2 — Country villages and the lake:** Start in Ballard, explore Los Olivos, then head toward Lake Cachuma. If you want a longer boat rental or the coolest outdoor temperatures, visit the lake first and reverse the day. Keep wine tasting optional and have a sober driver.

**With extra time:** Los Alamos is another worthwhile valley town for food and browsing; it is outside this short video's core route.
