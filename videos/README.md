# Santa Ynez & Beyond

[Download the 60-second video](https://github.com/thebenwalther/playground/raw/refs/heads/santa-ynez-video/videos/santa-ynez.mp4)

A widescreen visitor guide featuring a stylized animated 3D map, captions, and original instrumental music. This download is 1280 × 720, H.264/AAC, approximately 4.7 MB.

Highlights include Santa Ynez, Ballard, Solvang, Los Olivos, Buellton, and Lake Cachuma. The geography is illustrative and not to scale.

See [the visitor guide](Visitor-Guide.md) for the suggested two-day itinerary and planning links. Confirm current opening hours, reservations, and access before traveling.
